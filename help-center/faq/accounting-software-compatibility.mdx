## Compatibility

SkyITVPS supports major accounting platforms such as 1C, QuickBooks, and Sage.

## How to Check

1. Contact support with your accounting software name and version.
2. Most Windows-based and web apps work out of the box.
3. We provide pre-installed images for popular accounting solutions.

## Tips

- Custom software may require additional configuration.
- Data security and speed are standard across all server types.
