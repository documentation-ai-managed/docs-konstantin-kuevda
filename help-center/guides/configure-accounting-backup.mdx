---
title: Set Up Automatic Backups for Accounting Data
description: How to schedule daily backups for your accounting software data on SkyITVPS.
---

## Why Backups Matter

Protect your critical accounting data from loss or corruption.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to Server">
    Connect via RDP (Windows) or SSH (Linux) to your SkyITVPS instance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule Backup Task">
    Use Windows Task Scheduler or `cron` for Linux:
    ````bash
    # Linux example
    0 2 * * * tar czf /backup/accounting-$(date +%F).tar.gz /path/to/data
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Storage">
    Ensure /backup or backup disk has enough space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test Restore">
    Try restoring a backup to confirm integrity.
  </Step>
</Steps>
